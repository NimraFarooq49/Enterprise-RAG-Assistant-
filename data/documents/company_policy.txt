Company Name: BrightWave Technologies

Leave Policy:
All full-time employees are entitled to 18 paid leaves per calendar year. Out of these, 10 are casual leaves and 8 are sick leaves. Unused casual leaves can be carried forward to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Sick leaves cannot be carried forward.

Work Hours:
Standard working hours are from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Employees are required to complete 8 working hours per day, with a 1-hour lunch break.

Remote Work Policy:
Employees are allowed to work remotely up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval. Remote work requests must be submitted at least 24 hours in advance through the HR portal.

Probation Period:
All new employees undergo a probation period of 3 months from their date of joining. During probation, employees are entitled to 1 paid leave per month. Confirmation of employment is subject to a performance review at the end of the probation period.

Resignation Policy:
Employees who wish to resign must submit a written notice of at least 30 days. During the notice period, employees are expected to complete a proper handover of their responsibilities. Early release from the notice period is subject to manager and HR approval.

Reimbursement Policy:
Employees can claim reimbursement for official travel, internet bills up to Rs. 2000 per month, and client meeting expenses. All reimbursement claims must be submitted within 15 days of the expense, along with valid receipts.

Company Address:
BrightWave Technologies, 4th Floor, Tech Tower, Shahrah-e-Faisal, Karachi, Pakistan.
