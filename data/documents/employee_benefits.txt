# BrightWave Technologies

## IT & Employee Benefits Policy

### Health Insurance

All full-time employees are eligible for company health insurance after completing 30 days of employment. The company covers 80% of the employee's premium, while employees are responsible for the remaining 20%.

### Internet Allowance

Employees working remotely can claim an internet allowance of up to Rs. 3000 per month. Claims must be submitted through the HR portal with a valid internet bill.

### Laptop Policy

Employees who require a company laptop must submit a request to the IT department. Company laptops are provided based on job requirements and manager approval.

### Training and Development

Employees can attend up to 3 company-sponsored professional training programs per calendar year. Training requests require manager approval and should be submitted at least 7 days before the training date.

### Performance Reviews

Employees receive a formal performance review twice a year. Performance reviews are generally conducted in June and December.

### Public Holidays

The company observes official public holidays announced by the Government of Pakistan. Employees will be notified of the holiday schedule by HR.

### IT Support

Employees can report technical issues to the IT support team through the company's internal support portal. Urgent IT issues should be reported immediately.

### Employee Referral Program

Employees who successfully refer a candidate who is hired and completes 3 months of employment may receive a referral bonus, subject to company policy and approval.
